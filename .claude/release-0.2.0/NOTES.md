# Manor 0.2.0

Manor is now a native Mac app, rebuilt from the ground up in SwiftUI and AppKit. It opens faster, uses less memory, and feels at home on macOS, with Liquid Glass on macOS 26 and deliberate light and dark themes.

## New

- **Alfred, your personal assistant.** Alfred lives in the menu bar and drops from the notch with Control-Option-Space. General mode answers anything you type or anything on your screen. Prep mode listens to a call and prepares answers as questions end. Answers can include step-through diagrams and native charts and tables.
- **Alfred inside Manor.** With Manor in front, Control-Option-H opens Alfred in the window. Ask about your day or tell it what to change: check off tasks, move them to another day, log habits, start a focus session, move a role to its next stage, or add to the open note. Every change shows with an Undo button, and anything that can't be undone asks first.
- **Write with Alfred in Notes.** Type /alfred to open the composer, add images if you like, and Alfred writes into the note as real headings, lists, tables, callouts, diagrams, and views. Ask it to change the result, try again, or turn it into regular blocks.
- **Edit a selection with Alfred.** Select text or blocks and press Command-J to improve, shorten, simplify, or turn it into a list, table, or diagram. You see a preview first and choose Replace or Insert below.
- **Tracking** brings habits, mood, and focus together, with a daily check-in and a history of every month.
- **Jobs** has a pipeline board and a listings view.
- **Home** adds a Master view of every task, grouped by context.

## Improved

- Every date, time, and choice menu now uses Manor's own calendar, time list, and searchable lists, with typed dates like "fri" or "next week", in light and dark.
- The sidebar matches the system's: the window buttons sit inside it, rows use plum symbols, and the selected page reads at a glance.
- Notes, Problems, Pomodoro, Bookmarks, and Settings were rebuilt natively with the same data and flows.

## Changed

- The AI-written daily synthesis and the weekly reviews page are gone. The evening debrief is now a quick daily rating of mood and focus.

## Before you update

Manor 0.1 can't install 0.2 by itself. Download Manor 0.2.0 from the release page and drag it to Applications once; later updates install automatically. Alfred uses your own keys: add an OpenAI key (and a Deepgram key for Prep mode) in Settings, under Alfred.
